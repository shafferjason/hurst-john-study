# John Recap Video — Storyboard (v1, 2026-10-31)

Locked with Jason 2026-10-31:
- Lives on the study website only. Stands on its own: covers the LESSONS, not the songs. No song art, no song audio, no song titles or lyrics as on-screen text (Jason, 2026-10-31: "the main focus needs to be the lesson, not the song"). Every line must trace to the lesson write-up or the passage; if a phrase also happens to be a song title, prefer the lesson's own wording.
- No narrator. Instrumental music (new Suno track, warm acoustic guitar + soft piano, gentle pulse) + playful kinetic words + drawings.
- Target ~3:00 (a little under or over is fine). 16:9, 1920x1080.
- Tone: playful with words/drawings, reverent with substance; quieter treatment for heavy lessons (24, 31).
- Ben credited once in the opening ("taught by Ben"); not named in lesson scenes.
- Built to grow: gold path shows all 21 chapters of John, covered stretch lit, rest dim; ends "to be continued". Re-render every few lessons.
- Style: gold (#d4a574 / #f0d4a8) self-drawing line art on dark leather (#0f0f0f), Georgia serif — matches the site.

Design rules (from research — Mayer: modality/coherence/signaling/segmenting; kinetic-type practice):
- 3–7 words on screen per beat; the drawing carries the idea.
- Words ARE part of the picture (shrink, burn, fill, drop).
- The path/chapter marker is always visible = structure signal.
- One idea per scene; cut on music beats.
- Every on-screen line must be grounded in that lesson's summary on the site (data.js). No invented claims.

Order = teaching order (lesson 22 sits under John 6 where it was taught, though its scripture is John 8).
